Before You Write

✦
What level of fitness do the tours require? +

Most tours involve walking on uneven ground — boreens, clifftops, boggy terrain. You don't need to be an athlete, but you should be comfortable walking for two to four hours with breaks. The Skellig Michael visit requires climbing 600 steps. Let me know your physical considerations and I will plan accordingly.

Do I need prior knowledge of Irish history or mythology? +

Not at all. Many people arrive with no background and leave with a relationship to the material that surprises them. When you book, I send a reading list — three or four short books and some poems — that primes the journey. Reading is optional but recommended.

Can you help with Irish ancestry research? +

Yes, and this is one of the things I most enjoy. If you have a family connection to a particular county or townland, I can incorporate it into the journey — locating old family land, visiting parish records, finding the place your ancestors left. Tell me what you know and we will go looking together.

How far in advance should I book? +

Summer and solstice departures fill 6–9 months in advance. Spring and autumn are sometimes available with 2–3 months notice. I keep a waiting list for cancelled places. If you have a specific date in mind, write as soon as you can.
